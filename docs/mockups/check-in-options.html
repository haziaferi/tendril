Q4·b — a CHECK_IN block on the Journal page (no schema)

CHECK_IN · mood 4 · 14:32

CHECK_IN · energy 4 · 14:35

Slept badly, but the walk helped.

PARAGRAPH

# 19 Sep 2026
> Mood: good · 14:32
> Energy: high · 14:35
Slept badly, but the walk helped.
Travels with the page (no new file, no merge code) — but it is a block: it sits in the block list under the row, lands in the page's FTS row and its Markdown export, and a block can be dragged, deleted or duplicated like any other. The Today strip's reason for being live rows, not blocks, cuts the other way.
