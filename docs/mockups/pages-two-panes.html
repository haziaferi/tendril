Pages Two Panes

14c on a wide window: the tree at 280 dp beside the open page, and three ways to organise its chrome — the tab's name, the tree's own controls (new page, collapse) and the four actions (search, journal, view-only, more) — each borrowed from a desktop notes app that already solved it. Click rows, expand Journal, drag the handle, collapse with the chevron or Ctrl+\, close a page with Esc. Drawn at the scale the app will use — every size below is dp × scale.

A · one bar, segmented — Apple Notes, Ulysses

One 52 dp bar; the left segment is exactly the tree's width and holds what acts on the tree — the collapse chevron at the far left, the name Pages, + — the right cluster holds the four tab actions over the page. No pane header, no second bar: the page's title is an h1 in the content, its menu folds into more. Cleanest, and the one that costs the most: the three detail screens give up their bar (the title becomes an inline field, the page menu merges with the tab's).

B · each pane its own — Obsidian, Bear

No global bar. The tree's 44 dp header carries what acts on pages — Pages, +, search, journal — and the chevron at its edge, next to the divider it collapses. The page's bar carries what acts on the page — its title, view-only, more (page menu + Trash). Collapsed, the page bar's leading slot takes the chevron and search/journal, so nothing is lost. Cheapest: the detail screens keep their bar; two of their actions move.

C · sidebar with a footer — Things, Craft

The tree has no header at all: chips, then rows, then a 44 dp footer with + New page as a word, search, journal and the chevron. The page's bar as in B. The sidebar reads as a list first; creation and finding sit where the hand rests at the end of a scroll, as Things puts "+ New List". Same cost as B.

What all three drop

The doubled label — a "Pages" title over a "PAGES" header — and the tab bar over the page bar. Each control has one owner: the tree's controls sit on the tree, the page's on the page. Ctrl+\ and Esc work the same in all three.
