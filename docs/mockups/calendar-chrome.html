Calendar Chrome

L5 of the desktop audit: the Calendar's 184 px of chrome above the grid — the segmented view row, the layer chips, the grid's own ‹ › row — fold into one 52 dp bar, the bar every tab already wears; Tasks' two segmented rows and the Road Map's chip row fold the same way, so every screen's header is Pages'. The bar is also the window's title bar: Windows draws its caption buttons over the bar's right end (JetBrains Runtime's custom title bar — Notion Calendar's 46 px toolbar, measured, is exactly this), and the bar's ground across the rail is the drag area. The time grid takes Notion Calendar's measured proportions: hour 48 px → 45 dp, gutter 60 px → 56 dp, the all-day row at the 22 dp chip pitch. Two things are decided; two are shown as options below — the layers' home (frames L1–L3) and the pickers' form (C1–C3).
