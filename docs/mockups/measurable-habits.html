Measurable Habits

§0.10 item 3 — a value on the habit's log entry. Ground measured on the phone (TickTick 7, Italian): a habit's goal is Realizza tutto (a check) or Raggiungere una certa quantità — a daily amount (3), a unit (Tazza), an amount recorded per check-in (1) and whether the check-in records it automatically; the row then reads '0 / 3 Tazza' and each tap on its icon adds one ('1 / 3 Tazza'); the detail carries a progress bar under the same fraction, and its statistics list Completamento mensile 1 Tazza and Totale completamento beside a monthly rate in % and a streak. B§10.3's rule (§0.6.6): presence, never absence — no fraction, no bar, no percentage, no chain by default. What is taken from TickTick: the unit, the amount per check-in, a repeatable check-in, the monthly and total amounts. What is not: the fraction on the row, the bar, the rate. The number the person sets, if any, is shown once and as a sentence. 1 px = 1 dp.

Proposed — a habit that counts: the row's check becomes + 1 cup, repeatable; the meta says what was done today

The row: a + disc in the checkbox's own slot (the critique's #1 — 28 dp under a pointer, 40 in a 48 dp target on the phone, so titles stay aligned), the day's total as the meta (2 cups today) where a plain habit shows its cadence. The pane: Counts (the unit and the amount a check-in adds) and You set (the optional number, a sentence — never 2 / 3); the presence gains the amounts — 2 cups today, 41 cups this month, on 12 days; the month of dots unchanged (a day with a check-in has a dot; the amount is not drawn as a size). Undo the last cup tombstones the last check-in, as undo does today.
