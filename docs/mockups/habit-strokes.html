Habits as brush-strokes on the calendar

The user (2026-09-21): a habit should not have a task's shape and never appear in the Month; on the grids it is a brush-stroke across its span of time — under the day's blocks, which may cover it — a reminder to keep that time for oneself. Decided in one batch: the span is the habit's time + duration (no duration → 30 min; no time → not on the grid); a soft-edged wash in the habit hue with the name along it in small caps, which fills after the check-in; Month never, the phone's dots lose the habit dot, Agenda and the Day's untimed list keep habit rows; the stroke is inert to drags and drops, a click opens the habit's sheet. Drawn at the app's tokens (Ink dark, Chalk light) on the Week grid's own geometry — 45 dp an hour, a 56 dp gutter, 44 dp day header, the all-day row.

What the mock says about the code

One painter under the blocks: WeekGridView and PlanView draw a lane's strokes first — the full lane width, a vertical gradient that feathers 6 dp at each end, the hue at 18 % (36 % once lastCompletedDate == today), the name in eyebrow in the habit hue (text once done, with a check) — then the blocks as today. timelineBlocks stops receiving habits; habitStrokes(habits, day) gives (habit, start, minutes) for habits with a time (duration or 30). The stroke is not a drop target (weekDropAt ignores it) and not draggable; a click on bare stroke opens the habit's sheet (the Tasks tab's HabitDetailSheet, shared). Month: MonthGrid and the phone's monthDots drop the habit kind; Layers ▾ keeps Habits and governs the strokes. Agenda and the Day view's untimed list keep their habit rows.
