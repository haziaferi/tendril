The scrim under a slide-over

The slide-over (14b) and the centred card (L6) draw their scrim as the text colour at 32 % (onSurface.copy(alpha = 0.32f)) — Material’s 32 % share, but on Material’s black scrim the share darkens; on the text colour it lightens the dark ground: Ink dark’s #1B1D21 becomes #58595C, a milky grey, and the panel (#1B1D21) then sits darker than the page it covers — the depth cue inverted. On a light ground the same rule darkens, as intended. The phone’s bottom sheet already takes Material’s black at 32 %. Grounds read from the shipped bundles [Verified 2026-09-21]: Notion overlayBackground #0F0F0F at 60 % light / 30 % dark (its side peek has no scrim at all — the page stays readable, which is why 14b chose a slide-over); Obsidian --background-modifier-cover #0A0A0A at 40 % dark, #DCDCDC at 40 % light; Material 3 black at 32 %. The proposal: one dark scrim, black, at a share under 32 % — the same colour in every register and mode, so the panel is always the lightest thing on a dark ground and the page still reads behind it. Shells at half scale; the strip at 1 : 1.
