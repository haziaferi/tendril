Drag Between Panes

B§13.6 #5 — a task dragged onto a day, an hour, or a Timeline day, the drop being the date write the Table or the Calendar already makes. Two homes: the Calendar's task tray (Sunsama's, Akiflow's backlog beside the week — the Plan view's rail grown into a pane) and the Timeline's No date rows. On the phone the tray is a strip under the Week and the Month.

  1. The tray lists what has no day (Unscheduled: no date, never steps) and what is past its day (Overdue); 280 dp beside the week on a wide window, collapsible and remembered; under Touch a strip under the strip. Nothing is placed for the person (§0.5.2) — the tray offers, the drag decides.
  2. The drop: on a day header or the all-day row → the date; on the grid → the date and the quarter hour under the pointer; a series asks this one or all? as its own drags do. The target slot lights under the ghost as it moves.
  3. Back to the tray: a block dropped on the tray loses its date and time (a series is refused, with a word).
  4. The Timeline: a No date row dragged onto a day column takes that day as its start (setDateCell — the same write the bar's drag makes).
  5. Under a pointer the drag starts on press-and-move; under Touch on a long press (the Plan view's rule). The ghost is the chip itself, tilted, with the target named.
