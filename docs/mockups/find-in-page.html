A · bar

The page shifts down by 44 dp while the bar is open; the field is full-width, so a long query is readable; the count sits beside it. The phone's form for free. Browsers, Bear, Apple Notes.

B · floating

Nothing moves; the card covers the top-right of the first lines — the eye and `···` under it. Familiar from editors. Needs a second form on the phone (the keyboard would push a floating card around).

What both do

Ctrl+F opens with the field focused and the selection as the query; ↵ next, Shift+↵ previous, wrapping; Esc closes and leaves the cursor on the current match. Matches are marks inside the text (the span transformation), so they survive editing; the count says 2 of 3, or No matches. Ordinary pages only — a Table has its filter, a canvas its search.
