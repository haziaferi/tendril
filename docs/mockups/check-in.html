§0.10 item 4 — a one-tap check-in, on the day

A note about the person, not a habit (B§10.3). It lives on the Journal's day page above the Today strip: five faces for mood, five bars for energy, either alone. A tap logs one check-in with its time; a tap on the filled one undoes it. The words are the page below. No score, no average, no chart — the month is colours where something was logged and nothing where not (Daylio's rule).
