Type Vocabulary

Ten named roles for every piece of chrome text in the app — one spelling per thing, enforced by the audit (no literal fontSize or fontWeight outside ui/theme/). The editor's content keeps its own sizes (body 16, H1 24 / H2 20 / H3 16 SemiBold). Inter at true 400 / 500 / 600; headers bold, not large.
