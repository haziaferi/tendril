Canvas Frames

§0.10 item 15 — frames (sections) on a canvas, and canvas templates. Ground measured live (Obsidian's Canvas, 125 %, the throwaway canvas): a group is a node of its own — {"type":"group","x","y","width":400,"height":400,"label"}, no parent field — drawn under the cards as a rounded rectangle with a 1 px border and a fill 3 % of the text over the ground, its label in a small pill above its top-left corner; created from the ground's context menu (Crea gruppo) at the click; a card dragged inside belongs to it by geometry alone, and dragging the group moved the card with it (both +199 units in the file). Deleting a group keeps its cards. JSON Canvas (item 6) writes exactly this node. Tendril's frame: CanvasNodeType.FRAME on the existing width / height / text columns — no schema change. 1 px = 1 dp.

At rest — a frame under three cards, one card outside

The frame is behind the cards (drawn first); its label is a 22 dp pill in label on dim above the corner; the fill 3 % of text, a hairline on faint (the critique's #1 — Obsidian's group border measures 4.6 : 1; border was 1.4), radius 12 (the family's largest — a frame is a region, not a card). The arrow between two cards inside it is unchanged.

Hover — the label is the handle

Under a pointer the label lights (text, a hairline) and shows its delete disc; the frame's body shows nothing — a press on the body pans the board as the ground does, so a large frame never swallows a pan on the phone. The label drags the frame and every card wholly inside it (Obsidian's geometry, measured).
