The Shelf

B§13.4 14h — a second pane beside the open page on a wide window: another page, this page's Road Map neighbourhood, or today's Journal. Tree 260 · page · an 8 px handle · shelf 380 (280–560, remembered). Ink at 1200 × 760.

  1. One header for the shelf: the kind's glyph, the title, ⇄ (put it in the main pane / open the full Road Map), × (close; Ctrl+Shift+\). A page in the shelf keeps its own bar as this header — no second bar under it.
  2. A page opened beside is the same editor as the main pane (editable). A page cannot sit beside itself; the shelf shows its graph instead.
  3. The graph is the Road Map's local graph at depth 1 (2, 3 from the header); a click opens the node in the shelf, a double-click in the main pane. Edges: mention dim, related the third hue; the centre node the selection tint.
  4. Today's Journal is the journal page with its Today strip; created if the day has none.
  5. Remembered: `pages_shelf` = `journal` · `graph` · `page:<id>`, and `pages_shelf_width`. Below 840 dp there is no shelf — the phone keeps its stack.
