§0.10 item 19 — a block selection, a block undo

The editor is one field per block, so a selection cannot span blocks and undo is the platform’s per field. Notion, Logseq and Craft answer this with a block selection mode beside the text selection [Assumed]: Esc from a field selects its block, Shift+↑↓ and Shift+click extend it, a drag on the ground draws a marquee, Ctrl+A twice selects everything; a selection takes Delete, Ctrl+C/X/V, Ctrl+D and a drag; Ctrl+Z undoes block operations. Obsidian gets all of it from its single text field. Frames a–e are the desktop at the pointer profile (Ink dark), f–g the phone, h the light ground. 1 px = 1 dp.

What the stack holds

Block operations only, per open page, in the session: delete (one block or a run), move (Move up/down, a group drag), indent / outdent, turn into, paste, cut. Each entry is the inverse — the blocks as they were, with their ids, orders and parents — replayed through the same DAO writes the verb used, under launchAndReindex so the FTS row and the page’s timestamp follow. Typing is not on it (the field’s own undo), nor a title edit, nor a property; page history (§0.6.13) stays the ten-minute safety net under all of it. Ctrl+Z with a field focused reaches the field; with none, the stack; the snackbar’s Undo is the same entry.
