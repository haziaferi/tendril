Habit Planner — the Habits tab

Phase 1 of docs/plans/2026-09-29-habit-planner.md. The planner's Day and Week views replace the Habits tab's Today and Week (V1); an interval habit sits in its block or under Any time today (V2); a tap selects an entry and docks the edit toolbar at the bottom (V3). Two directions for the Day view by time (V4), one for everything else. Colours are Ink's own, solved by paletteFor and hueColours; the five block hues come from Tendril's hue wheel, the planner's peach, gold, sage, lavender and sky mapped onto it (Q2). §0.5.2 holds everywhere: a done entry is marked; an entry not done is plain — never red, never "missed", never a fraction. Sample: the golden base-rules habits on Wednesday 1 October 2026, plus two interval habits. 1 px = 1 dp; the phone is drawn at full length rather than cropped to a screen.

Day by time — direction A, chosen (2026-09-29): blocks as sections, one line per entry

Revised after the first review (too much colour; the entries scattered). Each block is a heading — its hue as a small mark beside the name (the one place a block's colour appears, the person's rule), its name, its range, and · now on the current one. Every entry is the app's own habit row: the check leading (HabitCheck, filled in the accent when done, not the habit hue), the title, and a set time as a dim number at the row's end; one line each, at the timed-habit row's 48 dp (rowHeightDp - 8). Set times still come first in a block, in time order; the entries without one follow. No sub-labels, no cadence lines — the cadence is in the habit's sheet. So one left edge for every check, one for every title, one right edge for every time. Besides those marks, the only colour on the screen is the accent in the done checks.

Edit mode, per V3: a tap on a title selects the entry (a soft band, no border) and the toolbar docks at the bottom, 56 dp targets on a 64 dp bar, above the thumb and away from the rows. ↑ at the top of Midday goes to the bottom of Morning; ↓ at the bottom goes to the top of Afternoon. Skip, Pause and Move to Trash live under More, so none of the bar's one-tap buttons can remove anything. Done — or Back, or a tap on empty space — closes it. Counted: a move within the day is two taps, a move to another block or day three, a title change two.

Direction A on the desktop — two panes, the selected habit on the right

Same structure at the desktop's 29 px rows. The detail pane is the habit's, as it is today; the toolbar docks along the list pane's foot with its keys shown — ↑/↓ move, Ctrl+M Move to… (a bare letter is the list's type-ahead), Enter Edit, Esc Done — the 14e keyboard model extended, not replaced.

Day by time — direction B, not taken (2026-09-29): the day as bands, set times pinned, the rest as chips

The blocks become bands (neutral since the review: alternate bands on surface2, a faint 3 dp edge), their names in a gutter where Plan mode keeps its hours. Set times read as a short timetable; Any time entries wrap as chips, so a block with five flexible habits costs two lines instead of five rows. A now line crosses the current band. More of the day on one screen, and the day's shape is visible — at the cost of a narrower title column and chips that wrap unpredictably with long titles. Chips keep 44 dp; the check inside each is a 44 dp target.

Week by time — the desktop grid, the phone's day list

Editing the blocks

From the Day view's overflow, Edit blocks. A block runs from its start up to, not including, its end. Changing an end moves the next block's start with it; pushing past the next block's end is refused with the reason and the limit. The first start and the last end are free; times stop at 24:00. Weekday overrides sit under the block they change.

What Repeats offers

Every day · On some weekdays · Every few days · Every few weeks · Times a day · Times a week · Every few hours · Once

Times a day asks how many and, for each, a block or a set time (spread over the blocks is the default).
Every few hours asks the step, from and until; each time lands in the block that holds it, so Where is hidden.
Once asks the date.
Starts and Ends sit under More options, closed by default.

The planner's aliases — weekly, alternate days, alternate weeks — are not separate choices: they are On some weekdays, Every few days with 2, Every few weeks with 2.
