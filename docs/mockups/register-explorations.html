Register Explorations

Playground, Blush, Chalk and Kodachrome, each re-conceived by three apps’ colour structures. Every colour is derived from the register’s ground, text and hue by that app’s rule — a priority ladder, calendar colours, a type per colour, a paper-and-one-hue voice, covers, a grey ramp, channels — and solved to a contrast floor. Nothing is picked by eye; the structure is the variable.
