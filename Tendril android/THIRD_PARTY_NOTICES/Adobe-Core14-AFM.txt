Helvetica and Helvetica-Bold advance widths
===========================================

shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/tendril/app/domain/plan/export/PdfWriter.kt carries the advance
widths of Helvetica and Helvetica-Bold, read from Adobe's Core 14 AFM files (Helvetica.afm,
Helvetica-Bold.afm) and re-ordered by WinAnsi code. No AFM file is distributed; the widths are
the only part used, which is the modification noted here.
